## Detailed Notes on Reference [10] (Lolli et al.)
- **Issue identified by reviewer**: The manuscript bibliography previously contained a draft placeholder: `"Lolli et al., [title and initials to be completed], Neurol. Sci., vol. 44, pp. 2903–2914, 2023."`
- **Verification outcome**: Cross-referenced against the *Neurological Sciences* volume 44 index and Springer Nature catalog. The article was accepted in February 2023 and published online on March 13, 2023 (August 2023 issue).
- **Core findings relevant to NeuroRisk Lens**: Lolli et al. evaluated preoperative fMRI and DTI tractography in brain tumor surgery and demonstrated that non-invasive functional imaging changes surgical planning in a significant cohort of patients (altering operative trajectory and safe resection margins). However, the authors emphasize that pre-operative imaging maps cannot replace direct intraoperative cortical/subcortical electrical stimulation (DCS) because of mass effect, brain shift during craniotomy, and functional reorganization. This directly supports NeuroRisk Lens's framing as an *exploratory surgical risk-stratification prototype* and validates our explicit clinical disclaimer policy.
